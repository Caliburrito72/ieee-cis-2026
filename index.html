What Is IEEE CIS?

The IEEE Computational Intelligence Society Student Branch at SSN is a new community built to learn constantly, innovate, and grow beyond limits — powered by Neural Networks, Fuzzy Systems, and Evolutionary Computation. Open to all students, irrespective of IEEE membership.

🧠

Learn Computational Intelligence

Get hands-on with the ideas behind intelligent systems, from neural networks to evolutionary computation.

🚀

Build What's Next

Move from using AI tools to understanding and building them, alongside a community of peers.

🤝

Grow Together

Strengthen your technical and personal growth in a space open to every student, regardless of IEEE membership.
